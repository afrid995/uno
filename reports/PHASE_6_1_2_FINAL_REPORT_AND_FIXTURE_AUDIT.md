## 4. UNO Flip Fixture Audit Decision & Evidence

### Background
During recent fixture hardening, `FlipFixtureBuilder.injectPlayableFlipCard` was modified to set `activeColor` to the injected Flip card's color and clear `pendingActionState: null` when the match was in `AWAITING_STARTING_WILD_COLOR_SELECTION`.

### Audit Evaluation
- **Official Rules (Mattel GDR44):** When a Wild card is the starting card turned up, the player to the left of the dealer must choose a color before playing a card.
- **Engine Rules (`FlipMoveValidator`):** Rejects any card play action with `[PENDING_ACTION_REQUIRED]` while `AWAITING_STARTING_WILD_COLOR_SELECTION` is active.
- **Fixture Contract:** `FlipFixtureBuilder.injectPlayableFlipCard` specifically guarantees that the injected Flip card is legally playable immediately on the current top discard.
- **Decision:** The change is **fully intentional and correct**. When a test helper injects a playable Flip card onto an unresolved starting Wild discard, resolving the starting color to match the injected card simulates the designated player's color choice, satisfying the validator precondition and enabling immediate card play.

### Focused Regression Tests Added
Two deterministic tests were added to `packages/game-engine/tests/flip.fixture.test.ts`:
1. `resolves starting Wild state when target player already holds a matching Flip card (Path 1)`:
   - Starts from a valid 112-card starting Wild state (`pendingActionState = AWAITING_STARTING_WILD_COLOR_SELECTION`, `activeColor = null`).
   - Ensures the player holds a Flip card in hand.
   - Calls `injectPlayableFlipCard`.
   - Asserts: `activeColor` resolved to Flip card's color, `pendingActionState` is null, card is at hand index 0, 112 cards strictly conserved, and `FlipMoveValidator.validate` confirms legal playability.
2. `resolves starting Wild state when a Flip card must be located and swapped into target hand (Path 2)`:
   - Starts from a valid starting Wild state where the player holds 0 Flip cards.
   - Calls `injectPlayableFlipCard` (forcing inventory search and swap).
   - Asserts: Flip card located and swapped, hand size preserved, `activeColor` resolved, `pendingActionState` cleared, 112 cards conserved, and `FlipMoveValidator.validate` confirms legal playability.

Both tests pass with 100% determinism.
