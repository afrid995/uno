## 1. Executive Summary & Edition Selection

UNO Attack is an iconic, electronic action variant of UNO characterized by a motorized Card Launcher replacing the static draw pile. The launcher dispenses an unpredictable number of cards (from 0 to 12) whenever a player presses its launch button.

Over its multi-decade history, Mattel has published several distinct editions of UNO Attack:
1. **1998/2003 Classic Edition (Model 41940 / 41941 / T8219):** Contained 112 cards with legacy mechanics such as *Hit 2*, *Trade Hands*, *Discard All*, and *Wild All Hit*.
2. **2021 Mega Hit Edition (Model GXY81):** Introduced specialized *Mega Hit* and *Wild Mega Hit* cards with multi-target hit modifiers.
3. **2020–2024 Modern Definitive Edition (Model GTX66 / GTX66-0970):** The contemporary standard edition featuring streamlined high-energy action cards: *Hit 2*, *Wild Hit 4*, *Wild Attack-Attack*, *Discard All*, and *Wild Customizable* cards, with explicit rulings for multi-player targeting, opening-card resolution, final-card execution, and 2-player dueling.

### Selected Product & Authority

## 3. Official Rules of Play (Mattel GTX66)

### 3.1 Setup
1. A dealer is selected.
2. 7 cards are dealt to each player.
3. 1 card is flipped faceup from the deck onto the playing surface to form the initial **Discard Pile**.
4. The remaining cards are placed facedown inside the **Card Launcher** (forming the Draw Pile).
5. The launcher is turned to face the player to the dealer's left. Play begins with the player to the left of the dealer and proceeds clockwise.

### 3.2 Opening Discard Card Rules
If an Action or Wild card is turned up to start the Discard Pile, the following official rules apply:
- **Number Card (1–9):** Normal play begins with the player to the left of the dealer.
- **Reverse:** Direction of play reverses immediately. The dealer plays first, and play moves counter-clockwise (to the right).
- **Skip:** The player to the left of the dealer is skipped. Play begins with the second player to the dealer's left.
- **Wild Card:** The player to the left of the dealer calls out a color, then plays.
- **Discard All:** The player to the left of the dealer begins play with a card of the same color, or another Discard All card. If not possible, they must hit the Launcher button.
- **Hit 2:** The player to the left of the dealer must hit the Launcher button twice. Play begins with the second player to the dealer's left.
- **Wild Hit 4:** The player to the left of the dealer must hit the Launcher button 4 times. Play begins with the second player to the dealer's left.
- **Wild Attack-Attack:** The player to the left of the dealer selects any other player to "Attack". The attacked player hits the launcher button twice. Play begins with the player following that attacked player in turn order. The player to the left of the dealer also calls the starting color.
- **Wild Customizable:** The player to the left of the dealer chooses the starting color.

### 3.3 Normal Turn Procedure & The Turn-Ending Launcher Rule
On their turn, a player must match the top card of the Discard Pile by **number**, **color**, or **symbol**, or play a legal Wild card.

#### The Mandatory Turn-End Rule
- If the player has no playable card, or chooses not to play a card, they **must press the Launcher Button**.
- **Critical Official Rule:** Unlike Classic UNO, where a card drawn from the deck may be played immediately if matching:
  > *"In UNO® ATTACK, if cards shoot out when you press the button and one or more of them are playable, you MAY NOT throw them down on the discard pile. ONCE YOU PRESS THE LAUNCH BUTTON YOUR TURN IS OVER."*
- If no cards shoot out, the turn ends harmlessly and passes to the next player.
- If cards shoot out, the player collects all dispensed cards into their hand, and their turn is over.

## 4. Action Cards Behavior & Mechanics

### 4.1 Reverse
Reverses the direction of play. (Clockwise becomes counter-clockwise, and vice-versa).

### 4.2 Skip
The next player in rotation loses their turn.

### 4.3 Hit 2
- The next player in rotation must hit the Launcher button **twice** (press once, wait/settle, press second time).
- The victim collects all cards dispensed across both presses.
- The victim's turn is over; play advances to the player following the victim.

### 4.4 Discard All
- When played, the player may discard **all cards in their hand of the same color** as the Discard All card.
- The Discard All card is placed on top of the discard pile, establishing the active color and value.
- May be played on any card matching its color or on another Discard All card.

### 4.5 Wild Card
- May be played on any turn, regardless of hand contents.
- The player designates the active color that continues play.

### 4.6 Wild Hit 4
- The player designates the active color.
- The next player in rotation must hit the Launcher button **four times**.
- The victim collects all dispensed cards.
- The victim's turn is over; play advances to the player following the victim.

### 4.7 Wild Attack-Attack
- The player designates the active color.
- The player chooses **any other player in the game** to "Attack".
- The attacked player must hit the Launcher button **twice**.
- Play continues with the **next player in turn order after the player who got attacked**.
- **Crucial Official Rule (Page 3):**
  > *"If a player goes out using a Wild Attack-Attack card, they may use this command card as normal before the game ends."*
  The chosen victim must serve the 2 launcher presses before the round concludes and points are calculated!

### 4.8 Wild Customizable Card
- May be played on any turn; player designates the active color.
- In digital UNO Ultimate, serves as a standard Wild card with flexible house-rule theming.

## 5. Virtual Launcher Simulation & Architecture

### 5.1 Physical Mechanical Principles
In physical Mattel UNO Attack units, a battery-powered motorized rubber flywheel spins when the launch button is depressed. Cards are unpredictably pushed into the spinning roller by an internal cam and tension arm.
- **Tension Build-up (Stream State):**
  > *"If no cards fly out, the lights and sounds will PULSE FASTER AND FASTER each time it’s pressed...until BAM! When cards finally launch, the player who pressed the button must add them to their hand."*
- **Outcome Distribution:**
  - Zero-card outcome (silent pass): roughly 30%–45% of single presses when tension is low.
  - Few cards: 1 to 4 cards.
  - Large burst: 5 to 10+ cards.
  - Maximum mechanical burst capacity: up to 12 cards.

### 5.2 Server-Authoritative Simulation Policy
1. **Server Determinism & State:** The virtual launcher maintains an internal tension level (from 0 to 4).
   - Each press where 0 cards launch increases tension by +1, increasing the probability and potential burst size of the subsequent press.
   - When cards launch, tension resets to 0.
2. **Multi-Press Execution:**
   - Multi-press commands (*Hit 2* = 2 presses, *Wild Hit 4* = 4 presses, *Attack-Attack* = 2 presses, *UNO Penalty* = 2 presses) evaluate sequential button presses on the server.
   - All cards dispensed across the presses are aggregated and atomically transferred from `drawPile` into the target player's hand.
3. **Card Conservation Invariant:**
   - Total deck size ($112$ cards) is strictly conserved at every step:
     $$\text{drawPile.length} + \text{discardPile.length} + \sum \text{player.hand.length} = 112$$
4. **Draw Pile Recycling:**
   - If the launcher is empty or contains fewer cards than dispensed:
   - All cards from the Discard Pile except the top active card are shuffled and reloaded into the launcher.
5. **Deterministic Testing:**
   - The launcher accepts an injectable random generator (`rng: () => number`) or explicit fixture overrides to allow deterministic testing of 0-card, 1-card, and multi-card outcomes.

## 6. Going Out, Final-Card Action Resolution & Scoring

### 6.1 Calling UNO
- When a player has 1 card left, they must yell "UNO" before playing their next-to-last card.
- If caught by another player before the next player takes their turn, the penalty is **hitting the Launcher button twice**!

### 6.2 Going Out & Final-Card Resolution
- Under Mattel GTX66 "GOING OUT", once a player is out of cards, the hand is over and points are totaled.
- **Wild Attack-Attack Final Card Exception:** Official GTX66 rules explicitly state on Page 3: *"NOTE: If a player goes out using a Wild Attack-Attack card, they may use this command card as normal before the game ends."* Therefore, when Wild Attack-Attack is the final card played, the attacking player designates a target opponent who must complete their 2 launcher presses, collect any dispensed cards, and only then is round victory declared and scored.
- **Hit 2 and Wild Hit 4 Final Card Resolution:** Unlike Wild Attack-Attack, *Hit 2* and *Wild Hit 4* contain no such exception note in the official GTX66 rules. Under the general Going Out rule, when a player plays their final card (including Hit 2, Wild Hit 4, Skip, Reverse, Discard All, or Wild), the hand immediately ends without additional launcher presses by the next player. (Audited and confirmed in Phase 10.1).

### 6.3 Scoring Table
Points accumulate from all cards remaining in opponents' hands:
- **Number Cards (1–9):** Face Value (1 to 9 points)
- **Reverse:** 20 Points
- **Skip:** 20 Points
- **Hit 2:** 20 Points
- **Discard All:** 30 Points
- **Wild Hit 4:** 40 Points
- **Wild (Standard):** 50 Points
- **Wild Attack-Attack:** 50 Points
- **Wild Customizable:** 50 Points

### 6.4 Match Victory
The first player to reach **500 points** across accumulated rounds wins the game.

## 7. Two-Player Game Rules

When played with 2 players, official Mattel GTX66 rules establish:
1. Playing a **Reverse** acts like a **Skip**: the player who played Reverse immediately takes another turn.
2. Playing a **Skip** allows the player to immediately play another card.
3. When a **Hit 2** is played, the opponent hits the Launcher button twice, their turn is skipped, and play returns immediately to the attacker.
